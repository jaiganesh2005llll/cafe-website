    Our story

    We opened Brew & Bloom in 2019 with one grinder, six stools and a belief that coffee tastes better when nobody is rushing you.

    Our beans come from three farms we visit every year. We roast weekly in small lots, bake daily, and compost every scrap. Stay as long as you like.
